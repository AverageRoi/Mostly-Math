**Mostly Math** is a collection of mathematical thought experiments and calculations. The firestarter for articles is always on my notebook when I start wandering through my mind.

The point isn't really to turn every question into a lesson, but rather to just do some exposition on some mathematical fun facts! It's also supposed to be an easy read and show a line of thought through the evolution of each article.

The plots are computed directly in your browser using [JSXGraph](https://jsxgraph.org/) or a small Canvas fallback.
